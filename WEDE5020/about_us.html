About Us

Our Story

Founded in 2026 by Potego Ntsoane, PP Fitness Institute started as a passion project and grew into Tembisa's community hub for all ages and fitness levels.

Mission & Vision

Mission: Empower individuals through expert martial arts and fitness training, building a supportive community focused on discipline, health, and growth.

Vision: Become Tembisa's leading institute for combat sports and fitness, known for creating well-rounded athletes and confident people.

Meet the Founder

Potego Ntsoane, a lifelong fitness enthusiast and martial arts expert, helps others unlock their potential.
